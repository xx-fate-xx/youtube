# Short 1 — "How Fast Before Super Speed Kills You?"  (9:16, ~50 s)

Version 2 (2026-11-22): US units in narration (both systems on screen) and the new hand-over line to the long
video (v1 kept in script_v1.md). Parent: whatif_super_speed (rescues, "you're unbreakable, they're not").
Premise: super speed, but the body stays human.

VO: You get super speed, but your body stays completely human. So how fast can you actually go?

VO: At sixty miles an hour, one stumble means hitting the road at highway speed, with no helmet and no leathers.

VO: Let's say your feet never slip. At a hundred and eighty-five, the wind in your face is stronger than a
category five hurricane.

VO: At the speed of sound, the air pushes back with more than six thousand pounds, the weight of two cars, on a
body that was built for walking.

VO: And at three times that, the air piling up in front of you heats past nine hundred degrees Fahrenheit.

VO: The speed itself never hurts you. The air does.

VO: But what if you were invulnerable? Then you could save anyone... right? Full video, linked below.
