# Short 1 — "How Fast Before Super Speed Kills You?"  (9:16, ~50 s)

Parent: whatif_super_speed. Premise: super speed, but the body stays human. Ends by handing over to the long
video ("what if nothing could hurt you?").

VO: You get super speed, but your body stays completely human. So how fast can you actually go?

VO: At a hundred kilometres an hour, one stumble means hitting the road at motorway speed, with no helmet and no
leathers.

VO: Let's say your feet never slip. At three hundred, the wind in your face is stronger than a category five
hurricane.

VO: At the speed of sound, the air pushes back with more than three tonnes, the weight of two cars, on a body
that was built for walking.

VO: And at three times that, the air piling up in front of you heats past five hundred degrees.

VO: The speed itself never hurts you. The air does.

VO: So what if nothing could hurt you? Full video, linked below.
