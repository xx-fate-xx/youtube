# Research 4 — the 1910 flood and the clocks (researched 2026-11-21)

Sources:
- DRIEAT (SPC Seine moyenne-Yonne-Loing) centenary bulletins built from 1910 data: 21-01-1910 — https://www.drieat.ile-de-france.developpement-durable.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/bulletin_21-01-1910_cle06a199.pdf ; bilan — https://www.drieat.ile-de-france.developpement-durable.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/bilan_1910_cle7c8919.pdf
- Archives de Paris, "Repères chronologiques" crue 1910 (Wayback) — http://web.archive.org/web/20250613085621/https://archives.paris.fr/_depot_ad75/_depot_arko/articles/643/crue-1910_doc.pdf
- Le Petit Parisien, 23 Jan 1910 (quoted with RetroNews links by histoires-de-paris.fr) — https://www.histoires-de-paris.fr/inondation-usine-air-comprime-janvier-1910/
- Histoire de la SUDAC (1877–1996), annex 2 p.140 and p.56 — https://www.sudac.fr/sites/g/files/dvc4171/files/document/2021/03/Livre%20-%20Histoire%20de%20la%20Sudac%20%281877%20-%201996%29.pdf
- J. H. Jackson, Paris Under Water (2010) (snippet: "time itself came to a halt, at 10:53 PM").

Facts:
- Slow river flood, not a flash flood: Austerlitz gauge ~4.00 m (20 Jan), 4.76 m (21 Jan), 5.93 m (22 Jan), 7.09 m (25 Jan), peak 8.62 m at noon on 28 Jan 1910; back to normal around mid-March (high).
- ~20,000 buildings flooded (cellars/ground floors); metro lines stopped from 25 Jan; electric plants intermittently stopped 23–24 Jan (Archives de Paris, DRIEAT) (high). "Destroyed" is not supported.
- Clocks: on the evening of Friday 21 Jan 1910 all pneumatic clocks, public and private, stopped "dans la soirée de vendredi entre 10 h 48 et 10 h 53" (Petit Parisien 23 Jan 1910, medium-high); SUDAC: "Arrêt des horloges pneumatiques à 22h53" (high).
- Cause: the compressed-air plant at 1, quai de la Gare (13th arr.) was flooded (Petit Parisien; SUDAC; Archives de Paris) (high). Lifts and air motors stopped too.
- Service restored 3 February after 13 days (SUDAC p.140, medium-high).
- Only pneumatic clocks stopped, not "all clocks"; it was night, not morning.

Competitor: "flash flood 21 Jan" partly wrong; "buildings destroyed" overstated; "all clocks stopped at exactly 10:50" wrong (pneumatic clocks, evening, 10:48–10:53).
