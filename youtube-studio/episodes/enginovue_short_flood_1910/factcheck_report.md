## Confirmed
Friday 21 Jan 1910; only pneumatic clocks stopped; nearly thirty years of one pulse a minute (since 15 Mar 1880);
clock air from the quai de la Gare plant in 1910; Seine rising ~1 m a day; plant flooded that evening; stop between
10:48 and 10:53 pm; peak 8.62 m at Austerlitz on 28 Jan; ~20,000 buildings flooded; service back 3 Feb after
13 days (single source: SUDAC).

## Caveats
Rain on the night of 21 Jan is shown (render effect) but not sourced; low risk.
