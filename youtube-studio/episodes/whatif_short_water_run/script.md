Facts (parent factcheck_report.md, row 27 — Minetti et al. 2012, PLoS ONE, citing Glasheen & McMahon 1996):
- A human would need to slap the water at > 30 m/s (> 65 mph) with ~15× the muscle power available.
- The basilisk lizard runs on water by slapping and stroking the surface; the air cavity under the foot must not
  close before the foot is pulled out (Glasheen & McMahon; summarised in the same secondary source).
- The long video's rescue 5 uses water running to cross the Bay of Bengal ahead of the 2004 tsunami.
